Never look at a partial eclipse unaided.
Even 99% covered will burn your retina, painlessly and permanently. Use ISO 12312-2 eclipse glasses, welding glass shade 12+, or pinhole projection onto paper. Sunglasses, phone screens, smoked glass and exposed film are not filters. Only during total eclipse, and only while totality lasts, is the naked eye safe.
